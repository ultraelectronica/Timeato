Timeato alarm tones (alarm_argon.ogg, alarm_carbon.ogg, alarm_helium.ogg,
alarm_krypton.ogg, alarm_neon.ogg, alarm_oxygen.ogg) are the Android Open
Source Project material alarm sounds, fetched from
https://github.com/aosp-mirror/platform_frameworks_base
(path: data/sounds/alarms/material/ogg/).

They are licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0
(http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). No changes were made to the
audio files apart from renaming them to Android resource-safe names.

Default alarm: alarm_helium.
